How to Play

A team and an era (1980s–2020s) are drawn for you automatically after every pick.

Each draw shows that team's top four at every position plus its defense, with each player's best real season in that era. Pick one to fill an open slot.

Both reels are fully random — repeat teams and eras are part of the game.

Don't like a draw? Once per game you can hit New Team (era stays locked) and once New Era (team stays locked).

Classic shows each player's best season. Do You Know Ball? hides the stats entirely.

Fill all five slots, then play the season — once. Your five picks combine into a team rating out of 100, measured against the greatest possible all-time lineup. Hit the hidden bar and you go 17–0; everything below scales down. There's more than one perfect roster — but the reels decide who you get.

Built from real historical box scores. A handful of players and defenses are placeholder approximations.
